About the shop

Our shop, our word.

My uncle started buying scrap in this neighbourhood in the late 1990s. I used to sit on the weighing stool as a kid and watch him count out money without arguing over a single kilo. That is the way we still work today.

Most of our customers are repeat families and shopkeepers around Musheerabad. They call us when the old fridge finally stops, or when a renovation leaves a pile of cable behind.
